Lời Bản — Offline Edge AI Speech-to-Speech Translator

100% offline Android speech-to-speech AI assistant enabling real-time bidirectional Vietnamese ⇄ Hmong (MVN) interpretation in remote highland regions without cellular connectivity.

Problem
Highland field officers in Cao Bằng face Vietnamese–Hmong language barriers where 4G/Wi-Fi is absent and cloud speech APIs are unusable.
Decision
Packaged a 3-stage on-device pipeline into one Android APK: Streaming Zipformer ASR (INT8), QLoRA-tuned Qwen2.5-1.5B (GGUF Q4_K_M on llama.cpp), and VITS Piper TTS trained on VOV4 audio.
Proof
21.50% WER / 14.90% CER (~50ms ASR), 19,454 bilingual pairs preserving legal code-switching (VNeID), and natural 22.05 kHz offline TTS.
